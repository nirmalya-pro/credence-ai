# ROUND 3 DEVELOPMENT ROADMAP — CREDENCE AI

This document details the three strategic pillars planned for Round 3 development to scale Credence AI from a textual prototype into an enterprise-grade brand governance platform.

---

## 1. MULTIMODAL BRAND INTELLIGENCE

### Scope & Capabilities
- **Visual & Graphic Asset Verification:** Inspection of marketing banners, infographics, packaging renders, and social media imagery.
- **Logo Integrity & Clearspace Compliance:** Automatic verification that corporate logos adhere to approved aspect ratios, colorways (e.g. CMYK/RGB/HEX), and minimum isolation spacing.
- **Color Palette & Contrast Auditing:** WCAG 2.1 AA/AAA compliance checking for legibility of text against graphic backgrounds.
- **OCR Text Extraction & Image Claim Verification:** Extraction of embedded text inside images to check claims, nutritional panels, and promotional badges against the Brand Truth Ledger.

### Why It Matters
Modern brand damage rarely happens through plain text alone; off-brand typography, outdated product mockups, and illegible disclaimers in display ads are the most common vectors for brand erosion.

### How It Extends the Concept
Extends Credence AI from text pre-flighting into complete multimodal omni-channel governance.

---

## 2. VERSION-AWARE BRAND TRUTH LEDGER & IMPACT SCANNING

### Scope & Capabilities
- **Automated Affected-Content Identification (Reverse Impact Scanning):** When a brand fact or price is modified in the Truth Ledger (e.g., product price changes from ₹1,199 to ₹1,299), Credence AI automatically scans previously approved marketing assets and alerts marketing teams about now-outdated live campaigns.
- **Source Expiry & Deprecation Timers:** Ability to set expiration dates on certified facts (e.g., promotional licensing rights or seasonal certifications) with automated warnings 30 days before expiration.
- **Git-like Branching for Brand Mergers:** Branching and staging of brand guideline changes during corporate rebrands before merging them into the production truth ledger.

### Why It Matters
Organizations currently lose control of their published content after approval. When prices change, old marketing collateral remains live online, causing customer friction and pricing disputes.

### How It Extends the Concept
Transforms the Brand Truth Ledger from a passive reference database into an active, retroactive compliance sentinel.

---

## 3. COLLABORATIVE BRAND GOVERNANCE & ENTERPRISE AUDIT

### Scope & Capabilities
- **Granular Role-Based Access Control (RBAC):** Distinct roles for Agency Copywriters, Brand Compliance Leads, Legal Counsel, and Medical/Scientific Reviewers.
- **Contextual Commenting & Threaded Discussions:** Enable creators and reviewers to debate flagged issues directly within the audit view.
- **Enterprise Single Sign-On (SSO):** Integration with Okta, Azure AD, and Google Workspace.
- **Guideline Friction Diagnostic (AI Policy Clarifier):** Analyzes recurring violations to automatically recommend clearer, less ambiguous rewording for confusing corporate guidelines.

### Why It Matters
In enterprise workflows, marketing involves dozens of external agencies and internal stakeholders. Fostering smooth communication while maintaining an immutable legal audit trail is vital for enterprise adoption.

### How It Extends the Concept
Matures the single-user prototype into a distributed, multi-agency collaborative operating system for enterprise brand safety.
